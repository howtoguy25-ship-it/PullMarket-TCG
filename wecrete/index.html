Finishes & Specs

Pick your finish.

The finish sets how your concrete looks and how much grip it has. Here's how we usually match them to the job.

FinishLookBest for
SwirlOverlapping circular trowel marks with good gripDriveways, paths and sloped areas
BroomFine parallel lines brushed across the surfacePaths and outdoor areas that need extra grip
Smooth trowelDense, smooth surfaceGarage floors and undercover slabs
ColouredColour such as charcoal mixed through the concreteDriveways and side access, with any of the finishes above

Residential driveways are commonly poured at 25–32 MPa with steel reinforcement mesh. We set the strength, thickness and reinforcement in your quote to suit the job and any engineering or council requirements.

How it works

From quote to cure.

Quote

We look at the site, measure up and send you a written quote with the scope, finish and price.

Prep

We excavate, compact the base, set the formwork, lay the reinforcement and fit any drainage.

Pour & finish

We pour, screed, float and finish, then cut control joints to manage cracking.

Cure

Keep foot traffic off for 24–48 hours and cars off for at least 7 days. We'll give you aftercare tips.

Policies

The fine print,
in plain words.

These apply to every job unless your written quote says otherwise. Last updated: November 2026.

01Quotes
  • We quote in writing after seeing the site or reviewing photos and measurements.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days. Concrete and material prices can change after that.
  • The quote lists exactly what's included. Anything not listed is extra and is agreed before we do it.
02Booking & Payment
  • Your job is booked once you accept the quote.
  • Any deposit and the payment schedule are set out in your quote.
  • Unless agreed otherwise, the balance is due when the job is finished.
03Changes & Variations
  • Changes to size, finish or colour are agreed in writing (a text is fine) before we do the work, with any price change.
  • Problems found during excavation, such as rock, tree roots, poor ground or buried services, can add cost. We'll stop and talk to you before going ahead.
04Weather
  • We don't pour in heavy rain or conditions that would damage the finish.
  • If weather moves your pour, we reschedule at no charge and let you know as early as we can.
  • In hot weather we may start early to give the concrete the best chance.
05Site Access & Preparation
  • Please move cars, bins and anything in the work area before we arrive.
  • We need access for a concrete truck or pump. Tell us about narrow gates, steep slopes or overhead wires.
  • Keep pets and children inside while we work.
  • Let us know about any private pipes, cables or sprinklers you know of in the work area.
  • Driveway crossovers and footpaths usually need council approval. We can tell you what's needed.
06Curing & Aftercare
  • Keep foot traffic off for 24–48 hours.
  • Keep cars off for at least 7 days, and heavy vehicles off for 28 days.
  • In hot weather, lightly hosing the slab for the first few days helps it cure.
  • Don't pressure-wash new concrete in its first month.
07Cracking & Colour
  • Concrete shrinks as it cures, so fine hairline cracks are normal. We cut control joints to guide where they form.
  • Coloured concrete can vary slightly between truckloads and settles in colour over the first few weeks.
  • Swirl and broom finishes are hand-worked, so small variations in pattern are part of the look.
08Workmanship
  • We stand behind our work. If something isn't right, contact us and we'll come and look at it.
  • Warranty terms for your job are set out in your written quote, in addition to your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
09Safety
Wet concrete is caustic. It can burn skin and eyes, sometimes without pain until hours later.
  • Stay out of the work area while we're pouring and finishing.
  • Keep clear of the concrete truck, pump and hoses.
  • If wet concrete gets on your skin, wash it off with clean water straight away. If it gets in your eyes, rinse for at least 15 minutes and get medical help.
10Clean-up & Environment
  • Concrete washout never goes into stormwater drains, gutters or gardens.
  • We remove our forms, offcuts and rubbish when the job is done.
11Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to quote, book and invoice your job: your name, phone number, email and job address.

  • We use it to contact you about your job.
  • We don't sell or share it, except where the law requires.

FAQ

Good questions.

How much does a concrete driveway cost?

It depends on the size, access, how much prep is needed, thickness, finish and colour. Call or text or send the quote form and we'll give you a written price.

How much concrete do I need?

Multiply length × width × thickness, all in metres. A 100 mm slab is 0.1 m thick. Add about 10%, or use the calculator.

Which finish is best for a driveway?

Swirl is our most popular. It has good grip for cars and feet and hides everyday marks well. See examples in Our Work.

When can I walk or drive on it?

Walk on it after 24–48 hours. Drive cars on it after at least 7 days. Heavy vehicles should wait 28 days, when the concrete reaches its design strength.

Do I need council approval?

Work on your own property usually doesn't. A new driveway crossover or footpath usually does. Ask us and we'll tell you what's needed for your job.

Will my concrete crack?

All concrete can develop fine hairline cracks as it shrinks. Good base prep, reinforcement and control joints keep cracking small and in the right places.

What happens if it rains?

We won't pour in heavy rain. We'll move your pour to the next good day at no charge.
