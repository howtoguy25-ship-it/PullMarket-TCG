About WeCrete

Poured right
the first time.

WeCrete is a ready-mix concrete supplier and delivery service built around one idea: the right mix, at the right site, at the right minute. We batch to order, dispatch to your window, and stay on the line with your crew until the last yard is placed.

Concrete doesn't wait — once it's batched, the clock is running. That's why every order is scheduled around your crew, your access and your weather, not ours.

Policies

The fine print,
in plain words.

These policies apply to every order unless your written quote or credit agreement says otherwise. Last updated: November 2026.

01Ordering & Scheduling
  • Please place orders at least 48 hours ahead. Same-day and next-day orders are accepted when trucks and plant capacity allow.
  • Every order is reconfirmed by phone by 2:00 PM the business day before the pour.
  • You are responsible for the quantity, mix and strength ordered. We are happy to help you check your numbers.
  • For multi-truck pours, we set the spacing between trucks with you in advance so concrete arrives at the pace your crew can place it.
02Cancellations & Changes
  • Cancel or reschedule free of charge until 2:00 PM the business day before your pour.
  • Later cancellations may incur a cancellation fee.
  • Once a load has been batched it cannot be cancelled and is billed in full, including any delivery charges.
  • Changes to quantity or mix after batching are treated as a new order.
03Weather
  • We watch the forecast with you. Pours may be postponed for heavy rain, freezing temperatures or unsafe conditions.
  • Weather reschedules requested before batching carry no fee.
  • Hot- and cold-weather adjustments (retarders, accelerators, heated water) are available and priced per yard.
  • Protecting fresh concrete from rain, frost and rapid drying after placement is the responsibility of the placing contractor.
04Delivery & Site Access

A loaded mixer truck can weigh up to about 70,000 lbs. Access must be firm, reasonably level and clear.

  • Plan for roughly 12 ft of width and 14 ft of overhead clearance, and keep the truck well clear of power lines.
  • The driver has final say on whether a route is safe and may decline unsafe access or positions.
  • Delivery past the curb line — onto driveways, lawns or private property — is at the customer's request and risk. We are not responsible for damage to pavement, curbs, landscaping or underground utilities, and you may be asked to sign a release.
  • You are responsible for permits, site preparation, forms, reinforcement and any traffic control required.
05Unloading Time & Standby
  • Each load includes a set amount of unloading time, based on its size, confirmed in your quote.
  • Time beyond the allowance — including waiting for the site, forms or pump to be ready — is billed as standby time in 15-minute increments.
  • Concrete must be fully discharged within 90 minutes of batching. If a delay at the site pushes past that limit, the load may be rejected for quality reasons and still billed.
06Water Added on Site

Adding water at the job site makes concrete easier to place, but it lowers strength and durability.

  • Water is added only at the request of the customer or their authorized representative.
  • The amount added is recorded on the delivery ticket and signed for.
  • Water added beyond the mix design voids any strength guarantee for that load.
07Short Loads & Returned Concrete
  • Loads under 4 yd³ carry a short-load fee, shown on your quote.
  • Concrete left over and returned to our plant may incur a return and disposal fee.
  • Drivers will place leftover concrete on site only where you have a legal, approved area for it.
08Pricing & Payment
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days. Prices can change with material and fuel costs; fuel and environmental surcharges may apply.
  • Cash customers pay when the order is placed or before the first load is batched. We accept major credit and debit cards and bank transfer.
  • Approved commercial accounts are invoiced on the terms in their credit agreement. Past-due balances may incur late charges and pause future deliveries.
  • Sales tax is applied where required.
09Quality & Limited Warranty

We warrant that our concrete meets the strength ordered when it is sampled at discharge and tested to ASTM C31 and C39 by a qualified testing agency.

  • We are not responsible for results controlled by others: subgrade, forming, placement, consolidation, finishing, jointing, curing and protection.
  • Hairline shrinkage cracking is a normal property of concrete and is not a defect.
  • Quality claims must be reported in writing within 30 days of delivery, with supporting test reports.
  • Our liability is limited to the cost of the concrete delivered.
10Safety
Fresh concrete is caustic. Prolonged contact with wet concrete can cause severe chemical burns, sometimes without pain until hours later.
  • Wear waterproof gloves, long sleeves, long pants, waterproof boots and eye protection.
  • If concrete gets on your skin or into your boots, wash it off with clean water promptly. If it gets in your eyes, rinse for at least 15 minutes and get medical help.
  • Stay clear of the rotating drum, the chute's swing path and the area behind a reversing truck.
  • Keep children and pets away from the pour area.
  • Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are available on request.
11Environmental & Washout
  • Concrete washout water must never go into storm drains, streets or waterways.
  • Drivers rinse chutes only into a designated washout area you provide or into our own containment.
  • Let us know in advance if your site requires a specific washout setup.
12Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to quote, schedule, deliver and invoice your order: your name, phone number, email, job-site address and payment details.

  • We use this information to run your order and to contact you about it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share it only with the service providers who help us operate — for example payment processing — and when the law requires it.
  • Payment card details are handled by our payment processor; we do not keep full card numbers.
  • You can ask us to review, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
  • This website does not use advertising trackers.

FAQ

Good questions.

How much concrete do I need?

Multiply length × width × thickness (all in feet) and divide by 27 to get cubic yards. A 4-inch slab is 0.333 ft thick. Add about 10% for waste, or use the calculator above.

Is there a minimum order?

We deliver loads as small as 1 yd³. Loads under 4 yd³ carry a short-load fee.

How far ahead should I book?

48 hours is recommended, and earlier for Fridays, weekends and peak season. Same-day orders are possible when trucks are available.

Can the truck drive on my driveway or lawn?

Sometimes, but a loaded truck is very heavy and can crack pavement or rut soft ground. Driving past the curb is at your risk. For tight or soft sites, a pump is often the better choice.

How long does the driver stay?

Each load includes unloading time based on its size. After that, standby time is billed in 15-minute increments. Having your crew, forms and tools ready keeps costs down.

What happens if it rains?

Call us before the load is batched and we'll reschedule without a fee. Light rain may be workable if your crew is prepared to protect the surface.

When can I walk or drive on it?

Generally, light foot traffic after 24–48 hours, passenger vehicles after about 7 days, and heavy loads after 28 days, when concrete reaches its design strength. Temperature and mix change this, so follow your contractor's advice.

How do I see examples of your work?

Our latest jobs are on Instagram at @wecreteconcreting. Message us there or call .
