Plans, usage & credits

NexaAi runs on a plan tier (Beginner/Pro/Max) plus a rolling usage window, and on prepaid credits for anything usage-based beyond your plan's included allowance. Credits are purchased in fixed packs shown at checkout and are deducted per real action taken (a chat reply, a voice turn, a who-is lookup, etc.) at the rate shown in the app at the time. We may adjust plan limits, credit costs, or pack pricing going forward; changes apply to purchases made after the change, not retroactively to credits you already hold.

Subscriptions & auto-renewal

Nova (Pro) and Zenith (Max) are auto-renewing monthly subscriptions. On iOS, payment is charged to your Apple ID account, and the subscription automatically renews for the same monthly price and duration unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period — your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from the Restore purchases / Manage subscription links on the Plans screen in the app. On web/Android, the same plans are billed by our payment processor (Paddle) on the same monthly cycle and can be cancelled from the account website's Settings page or by contacting support. Downgrading to Ember (the free/pay-as-you-go tier) takes effect immediately and stops future billing; it does not retroactively refund the current billing period (see Refunds below — we don't issue money refunds).

Refunds

We don't issue money refunds for credit purchases, subscriptions, or completed actions. Instead, NexaAi runs a real, automatic credit-back system for genuine billing errors: if a message is charged for and NexaAi never actually produces a reply, the exact credit charged is refunded to your balance automatically, immediately, with no request needed. If you believe a specific reply's charge was a genuine technical/billing error for some other reason, you can report it from that reply in the app ("Report issue") — a real backend review checks the actual facts of that one charge (whether a reply was generated, its length, what was charged versus what this app's own formula should have charged) against your description, and automatically credits your balance back if it finds a genuine error. This review never reads the content of your conversation — only the structural billing facts of the disputed charge. Every report, approved or declined, is logged with its real reasoning and reviewable by the account owner, who can manually approve one the automated review declined. This is a credit-balance adjustment only, never a cash refund to your original payment method.
